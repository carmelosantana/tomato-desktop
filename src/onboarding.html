Work in 15-minute windows.

Tomato runs your day in short focus sprints. You commit to one window at a time, then step away — no marathon sessions, no watching the clock.

Breaks that actually cost nothing.

When a sprint ends there's a short grace window to wrap up, then a real break. Your agents keep working while you're away, so stopping doesn't set anything back.
